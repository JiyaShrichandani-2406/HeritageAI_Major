CONTENT:

Chini ka Rauza is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Mughal-period tomb associated with Afzal Khan, a Persian poet and high-ranking official under Shah Jahan, near the Yamuna.

History of Chini ka Rauza. Official tourism information identifies Chini ka Rauza as the tomb of Allama Afzal Khan, also known as Mullah Shukrullah Shirazi, a poet-scholar and prime minister to Shah Jahan.

Architecture of Chini ka Rauza. Known for its Persian-influenced design and glazed tile decoration on the facade.
