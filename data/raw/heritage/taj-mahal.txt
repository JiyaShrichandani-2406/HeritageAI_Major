Taj Mahal is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Ivory-white marble mausoleum in Agra, built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

History of Taj Mahal. The Taj Mahal was commissioned by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan after the death of his wife Mumtaz Mahal in 1631. Construction began around 1632 on the south bank of the Yamuna River in Agra. The main mausoleum was largely complete by about 1648, and the surrounding complex, including the gardens, mosque and guest house, was finished around 1653. It was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983.

Architecture of Taj Mahal. A masterpiece of Mughal architecture that combines Persian, Islamic and Indian elements. The central mausoleum is built of white marble on a raised platform, with four minarets at the corners, a large central dome surrounded by smaller domed pavilions, and decorative inlay work. It stands at the end of a symmetrical charbagh garden, flanked by a red sandstone mosque and a matching building.
