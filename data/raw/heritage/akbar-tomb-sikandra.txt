Akbar's Tomb (Sikandra) is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Mausoleum of the Mughal emperor Akbar, located in Sikandra near Agra.

History of Akbar's Tomb (Sikandra). The official Agra district tourism page says the mausoleum was begun by Akbar and completed by his son Jahangir in 1613. It stands in a 119-acre charbagh at Sikandra.

Architecture of Akbar's Tomb (Sikandra). The five-storeyed sandstone-and-marble pyramid and its gateway combine Hindu, Islamic and Persian motifs, according to the official Agra district tourism page.
