CONTENT:

Jama Masjid, Agra is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Large Mughal congregational mosque opposite Agra Fort, commissioned by Jahanara Begum, daughter of Shah Jahan.

History of Jama Masjid, Agra. The Taj Mahal official nearby-attractions page describes the Jama Masjid as a major Mughal mosque opposite Agra Fort and notes its association with Shah Jahan's daughter Jahanara Begum.

Architecture of Jama Masjid, Agra. The mosque features a large courtyard and Mughal-era red sandstone and marble elements, with Iranian/Persian design influences described by official tourism sources.
