Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh) is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Ram Bagh, also known as Bagh-i-Gul Afshan, is a Mughal garden laid out by Emperor Babur around 1528 and regarded as the oldest surviving Mughal garden in India.

History of Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh). The Agra District Administration describes Ram Bagh as laid out by Emperor Babur around 1528 and as the oldest surviving Mughal garden in India.

Architecture of Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh). The garden has terraced walkways, water channels and pavilions overlooking the Yamuna, and is described by the Agra District Administration as a prototype of the charbagh tradition.
