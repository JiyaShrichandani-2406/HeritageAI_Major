Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh) is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Mughal garden in Agra, also known as Aram Bagh.

History of Ram Bagh (Aram Bagh). The garden is attributed to Emperor Babur and dated to 1526. Babur was temporarily buried there before his remains were moved to Kabul.
