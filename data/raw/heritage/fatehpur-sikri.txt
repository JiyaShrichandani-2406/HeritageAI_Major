Fatehpur Sikri is located in Fatehpur Sikri, Uttar Pradesh, India. Historic red-sandstone city in Agra district founded by Emperor Akbar in 1571 and used as the Mughal capital for about 14 years.

History of Fatehpur Sikri. The official Agra district tourism page states that Akbar founded Fatehpur Sikri in 1571 and that it served as the Mughal capital for about 14 years.

Architecture of Fatehpur Sikri. The complex includes Buland Darwaza, Jama Masjid, the marble Tomb of Sheikh Salim Chishti, Panch Mahal, Diwan-i-Khas and Jodha Bai's Palace. The district page describes a blend of Persian, Hindu and Jain architectural idioms.
