Roman Catholic Cemetery, Agra is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Historic cemetery in Agra containing European-era tombs and memorials, including the tomb traditionally associated with John Hessing.

History of Roman Catholic Cemetery, Agra. The cemetery is a recognized heritage site in Agra. The sources used for this dataset do not establish a complete official chronology, so individual dates and attributions should be checked against monument-specific records before publication.
