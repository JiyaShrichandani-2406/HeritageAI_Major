Agra Fort is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. The walled imperial city of the Mughals in Agra, rebuilt in red sandstone by Emperor Akbar from 1565 and later embellished in white marble by Shah Jahan.

History of Agra Fort. The official Agra district tourism page describes Agra Fort as the walled imperial city of the Mughals and states that Emperor Akbar rebuilt it in red sandstone from 1565, with later white-marble additions by Shah Jahan.

Architecture of Agra Fort. The fort's 2.5-kilometre ramparts enclose structures including Jahangir Mahal, Khas Mahal, Diwan-i-Aam, Diwan-i-Khas, Sheesh Mahal, Musamman Burj and Moti Masjid.
