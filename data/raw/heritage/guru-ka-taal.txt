Guru ka Taal is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Historic Sikh religious site in the Sikandra area, associated with Guru Tegh Bahadur.

History of Guru ka Taal. The Agra District Administration lists Guru ka Taal among Agra's historic and religious visitor sites and describes its association with the ninth Sikh Guru, Guru Tegh Bahadur.

Architecture of Guru ka Taal. The site includes a Sikh shrine complex and a historic water reservoir. Detailed architectural claims require confirmation from a dedicated heritage source.
