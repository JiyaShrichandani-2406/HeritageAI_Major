CONTENT:

Tomb of Itimad-ud-Daulah is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Mughal-era riverside tomb commissioned by Empress Nur Jahan for her father, Mirza Ghiyas Beg; often called the Baby Taj.

History of Tomb of Itimad-ud-Daulah. The tomb was commissioned by Empress Nur Jahan for her father Mirza Ghiyas Beg and was built between 1622 and 1628, according to official Agra tourism information.

Architecture of Tomb of Itimad-ud-Daulah. A compact Mughal tomb notable for extensive marble decoration and inlay work, with a garden setting beside the Yamuna River.
