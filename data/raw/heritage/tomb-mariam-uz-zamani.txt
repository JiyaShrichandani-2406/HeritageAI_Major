CONTENT:

Tomb of Mariam-uz-Zamani is located in Sikandra, Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Mughal tomb traditionally identified as the resting place of Mariam-uz-Zamani, wife of Emperor Akbar and mother of Jahangir.

History of Tomb of Mariam-uz-Zamani. The official Agra district tourism listing identifies the monument as the tomb of Mariam-uz-Zamani and places it near Akbar's Tomb at Sikandra.

Architecture of Tomb of Mariam-uz-Zamani. A pre-existing structure adapted as a tomb, with red sandstone, carved details and Mughal-era architectural features.
