CONTENT:

Mehtab Bagh is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. Mughal charbagh garden across the Yamuna River from the Taj Mahal, laid out originally by Babur and later aligned by Shah Jahan.

History of Mehtab Bagh. The official Agra district tourism page describes Mehtab Bagh as originally laid out by Babur and later aligned by Shah Jahan with the Taj Mahal across the Yamuna.

Architecture of Mehtab Bagh. The restored charbagh includes fruit trees and pathways, according to the official Agra district tourism page.
